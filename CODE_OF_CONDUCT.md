# Community code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss ideas and code with specific, constructive
feedback. Make room for different backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience.
Disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual attention,
personal attacks and publishing another person's private information are not.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed
spaces, and to people explicitly representing the project elsewhere.

Report concerns privately through the maintainer contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Include relevant links and the minimum information needed to understand the incident.
Maintainers should protect the reporter's privacy, hear the people involved where
appropriate, and explain moderation decisions to those affected.

Depending on severity and recurrence, maintainers may request a correction, remove
content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports
and appeals can use the same private channel. Immediate restrictions may be necessary
to stop threats or harassment while a report is reviewed.
